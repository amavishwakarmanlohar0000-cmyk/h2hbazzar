# H2H Bazaar — AI Builder Prompt

Create an **OLX-inspired online classified marketplace** named **H2H Bazaar (Hand2Hand Bazaar)** with the following specifications.

## Core Features
- **User signup/login** with email & phone verification plus optional social logins (Facebook/Google).
- **Profiles** with photo, bio, contact info, verification badges, and editable settings.
- **Ratings & reviews** after transactions (buyers rate sellers, optional seller feedback).
- **Ad posting** form with title, detailed description, price, category, condition, location (map + GPS/address), and contact info.
- **Media uploads** with image (and optional video) support plus real-time previews.
- **Pricing options** for fixed or negotiable pricing.
- **Boosting options** for premium/featured listings (extra fees).
- **Browsing & search** with category navigation (Vehicles, Real Estate, Electronics, Jobs, Services, etc.).
- **Search bar** with autocomplete and keyword suggestions.
- **Filters & sorting** by price range, location radius, condition, date posted, and seller type (individual/business).
- **Geo-targeted results** that prioritize nearby listings and allow users to change location/radius.
- **Map view** for search results (geo discovery).
- **In-app chat** between buyers/sellers with timestamps, image/document sharing, and notification alerts.
- **Privacy controls** to reveal contact details only after agreement.
- **Report/block** options for users and listings (spam/abuse).
- **Favorites & saved searches** with alerts for new matching listings.
- **Moderation** with automated + manual review, banned keyword detection, and AI checks for text/images.

## Advanced AI Enhancements
- **Personalized recommendations** using behavior signals (views, favorites, messages, searches).
- **Smart search ranking** that blends keyword relevance, recency, and engagement.
- **AI moderation** to flag scams, duplicates, and inappropriate content quickly.
- **Geo-targeted boosting** to promote listings near the user’s location.
- **Image recognition** to auto-categorize listings and detect prohibited images.
- **Pricing assistance** that suggests competitive price ranges based on market data.

## Responsive Design & Performance
- **Mobile-first, responsive** layout (phone, tablet, desktop).
- **Clean, modern UI** with minimal design, ample whitespace, and clear hierarchy.
- **Card/grid listings** with large images (OLX-like style).
- **Fast load times** with image optimization, lazy loading, and CDN support.
- **Sticky search/navigation** and touch-friendly controls.
- **App-like experience** with PWA support (offline cache + push notifications).

## Admin Dashboard & Analytics
- **User & ad management** with approval/removal, suspensions, and report queues.
- **Analytics dashboards** for signups, active users, listing volume, category performance, and conversions.
- **Moderation workflow** with audit logs and batch actions.
- **Monetization controls** for featured listings, pricing, and revenue reporting.

## Customization & Branding
- **Branding controls** for logo upload, primary/secondary colors, and font selection.
- **Layout controls** for homepage sections (latest listings, featured, categories).
- **Default theme** should be clean and neutral with a modern accent color.

---

**Instruction:** Implement the above to create a fully functional, intelligent classifieds marketplace platform inspired by OLX, optimized for UX, and enhanced with AI features.
